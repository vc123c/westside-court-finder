About this data. Collected Nov. 2–3, 2026. LA city locations come from the city's open data feed of Recreation and Parks facilities. Santa Monica, Culver City and Beverly Hills locations are their published addresses, placed with the US Census geocoder; Ocean View, Douglas, Fox Hills and Culver West Alexander are placed at their courts as mapped in OpenStreetMap. Every site links to its source page and opens in Google Maps.

OpenStreetMap additions. 15 more public parks and 48 public schools (LAUSD, SMMUSD, CCUSD, BHUSD and one charter) come from OpenStreetMap courts and fields, grouped by the park or school they sit in. Their court counts and lighting are as volunteers mapped them and have not been checked with each city. OSM may count each hoop or half court separately. Private schools, clubs, campuses and backyard courts are left out. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

Schedule updates (Nov. 3, 2026). Stoner's open gym became a paid club on Oct. 1, 2026 ($5/day or $32/month). Robertson's fall 2026 open gym is Tue/Thu/Fri 11:30 a.m.–1 p.m., $10/month. Rustic Canyon posts open gym Mon/Wed/Fri 12:30–2:30 p.m. on an undated flyer. Westwood's free Tue/Thu open play is confirmed for Sept. 1–Dec. 17, 2026.

Rules used. LA city outdoor courts: park hours 5 a.m. to 10:30 p.m. (LAMC 63.44); unlit courts end at sunset. LA city pay tennis: $8/hr weekdays before 5 p.m., $12/hr after 5 p.m. and weekends; only "open play" courts are free. Santa Monica: free drop-in tennis at Clover, Douglas, Marine and Los Amigos; Reed and Ocean View are reservation courts. Culver City parks are open dawn to dusk. Beverly Hills tennis is reservation-only, 24 hours ahead, no walk-ups.

Fields. No Westside public field posts free open-play hours. LA city turf fields rent for $50–70 an hour (2024 synthetic field list), and city adult leagues hold them Mon–Fri 7–10:30 p.m. and Sun 8 a.m.–5 p.m. At Cheviot Hills, grass fields and diamonds are booked through Municipal Sports on weekday evenings after 7 p.m. and on Sundays, and the center publishes its field calendar. No other Westside center links a field calendar, and none posts lights-off times; lit fields are shown open until the park closes (10:30 p.m. at LA city parks).

Gaps worth noting. Culver City lists no public indoor basketball gym. The LA County park needs assessment counts 0 gymnasiums and 1 basketball court in Beverly Hills, against 26 tennis courts. Westchester does not post hours. Palisades Rec Center is closed for programs after the Palisades Fire. Schedules change each season: call before you go.
